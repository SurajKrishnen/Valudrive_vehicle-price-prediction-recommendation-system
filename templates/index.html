Behind ValuDrive

How the system works

Price prediction and vehicle recommendation are combined into one machine-learning decision-support system.

01
◫

Vehicle Data

The system processes characteristics such as brand, model, year, kilometres driven, fuel type, transmission and technical specifications.

02
◇

Price Prediction

Random Forest Regression uses patterns learned from historical used-car records to estimate the expected resale value.

03
◎

Smart Recommendations

Weighted vehicle characteristics and cosine similarity identify comparable listings and suitable alternative vehicles.
